► WHAT IS ANOPHELYZE?

Anophelyze is building a patent-pending device that detects and incapacitates mosquitoes — and other disease-carrying vectors — in a way that is safe for humans and environmentally conscious.

No toxic chemicals. No lasers…well, maybe some. Just a smarter approach to one of the world's deadliest animals. Mosquitoes are crepuscular, most active at dawn and dusk — that's exactly when our device is on watch.

► WHY WE EXIST

Mosquito-borne diseases — malaria, dengue, yellow fever, Zika, and more — cause preventable death and life-disrupting illness across much of the world, hitting young children hardest. Historic control efforts leaned on toxic chemicals like DDT or had limited success.

Anophelyze was founded in 2026 by Camden Towne, after several years of preparation, out of a need for environmentally neutral approaches to disease-vector mitigation.
